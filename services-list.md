## 1. Elevadores de Escadas

**Also known as:** Cadeiras elevatórias, cadeiras elevatórias de escadas, stairlifts
**Primary URL:** `/equipamentos/elevadores-de-escadas/`
**Primary keyword:** elevadores de escadas
**Secondary keywords:** cadeiras elevatórias, cadeira elevatória, elevador de escadas preço, elevador escadas casa

### What it is
Equipment that allows a person to travel up and down stairs while seated, attached to a rail system fixed to the staircase wall or steps. Does not require major building work. Operates on household electricity with battery backup.

### Who it's for
People who have difficulty climbing stairs — whether due to age, injury, or reduced mobility — who want to continue living in their own home independently.

## 2. Elevadores Residenciais

**Also known as:** Elevadores domésticos, elevadores para moradias, home lifts
**Primary URL:** `/equipamentos/elevadores/`
**Primary keyword:** elevadores residenciais
**Secondary keywords:** elevador doméstico, elevador para moradia, elevador sem poço, elevador residencial preço

### What it is
A compact lift that connects two or more floors of a home. Can be installed without a traditional shaft in many cases (self-supporting structure). More complete solution than a stairlift, suitable for wheelchair users or where the stairlift is not appropriate.

### Who it's for
People who want full vertical mobility in a multi-floor home. Also for families planning ahead, or those who need wheelchair-compatible access between floors.

## 3. Plataformas Elevatórias

**Also known as:** Plataformas para cadeira de rodas, elevadores para cadeira de rodas, wheelchair platform lifts
**Primary URL:** `/equipamentos/plataformas-elevatorias/`
**Primary keyword:** plataformas elevatórias
**Secondary keywords:** plataforma elevatória para escadas, plataforma elevatória preço, plataforma cadeira de rodas, elevador para cadeira de rodas escadas

### What it is
A metal platform that travels along a rail on the staircase, designed to carry a wheelchair user (and chair) safely between floors. Different from a stairlift (which requires the user to transfer to a seat). Common in public buildings; also used in residential settings.

### Who it's for
Wheelchair users or people with very limited mobility who cannot or prefer not to transfer to a stairlift seat. Also widely used in condominiums, schools, commercial premises.

## Image Style Guide Reference

For AI-generated images (Gemini Flash / Nano Banana Pro), use this style:
- **Setting:** Portuguese residential homes — stone, tile, warm tones, urban apartments, older villas
- **People:** Older adults (65–80) using equipment naturally, family members nearby, dignity-forward
- **Camera imitation:** Sony A7R IV, 35mm f/2.0 lens, natural interior light, soft shadows
- **Mood:** Calm, warm, reassuring — not clinical or medical
- **Colors:** Warm whites, cream, terracotta accents, natural wood
- **Format:** WebP, not PNG or JPG
- **Avoid:** Stock-photo look, overly young models, sterile hospital environments, heavy saturation
